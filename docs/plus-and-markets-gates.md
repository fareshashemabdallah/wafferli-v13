# Dabberna Plus and multi-market release gates

These modules are **policy planning artifacts**, not functioning payments, subscriptions or country switches. Keep the customer-first family app useful without requiring payment while core financial/privacy services are being tested.

## Free pilot

Local family Money, tasks, family calendar/Plans, basic comparison, locally guided family advice and encrypted local backups are already working in the browser. They remain available in the current pilot. Never lock a parent's export/deletion access behind a paid plan.

## Proposed Plus, NOT sold

Future, *not yet implemented*: secure authenticated cross-device family data sync, grounded AI concierge, connected verified retailers/offers and richer shopping. No prices, trial periods, customer entitlements, taxes, refunds or billing plans have been announced. A local browser flag, query string, unchecked JWT or form choice can NEVER be the sole source of paid authorization. Production requirements include a server-generated entitlement ledger from verified payment-provider webhooks, authorization per request, cancellation and refund lifecycle, audited grants/revocations, household ownership and rate-limits.

## Jordan, Lebanon, UAE, Oman

Code contains a country configuration registry with currency metadata and expected weekends. It does **NOT** switch financial currency. All existing Money values, projections, payment-policy amounts and historic records remain **JOD**. It is unsafe to reinterpret amounts as Lebanese pounds, dirhams or Omani rials. Future launch needs separate money data versions, decimals, country-specific dates/holidays, provider calendars, tax, financial advice/privacy review, business registration and regulated PSP settlement. Never apply FX rates silently or convert balances without explicit verified user control.

Launch flags for paid billing, merchant settlement and additional countries are OFF. The About screen provides an informational preview only.
